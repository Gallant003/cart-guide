What to Look For

The best carts are caryophyllene-dominant, linalool-supported, and low in limonene. These terpenes keep things calm, smooth, and grounded — no jitters, no racing heart, no rocket launch. Binske Jungle Pie and Italian Wedding Cake are the gold standard examples.

Terpenes to Seek
Caryophyllene PRIMARY
Anti-anxiety, smooth heart rate, anti-inflammatory. This is your anchor — it needs to be first or second on the label.
Linalool
Calm, open mood, no brain fog. Works with caryophyllene to keep the headspace clear and relaxed.
Myrcene
Body relaxation and gentle onset. Slows things down in the best way — no sudden launch.
Bisabolol Humulene
Soothing secondary support. Grounding body feel without heaviness.
Terpenes to Avoid
Limonenewhen dominant
The jitter culprit. Fine as a background note — bad when it's #1 on the label.
Pinenewhen high
Overstimulation and racing thoughts. Avoid anything where this appears near the top.
Ocimenewhen dominant
Fruity rocket fuel. Too fast, too much, too anxious.
WHY ITALIAN WEDDING CAKE WORKS
High caryophyllene → anxiety reduction, smooth heart rate
Good linalool → calm, relaxed, open mood
Bisabolol + humulene → soothing body feel
Low limonene → enjoyable without the jitters
Low pinene → no overstimulation
Slow, gentle onset → no rocket launch
NORTH STAR
Binske · Jungle Pie
Clear, calm, zero anxiety, fully functional. The benchmark for everything else. If a new cart is this good, it passes.

Finding Carts Online

Dispensary websites are notoriously bad. Filters reset, menus are slow, and terpene info is buried or missing. Here's the least-painful way through them.

Best Sites to Use
Leafly
leafly.com ↗
Best for terpene research. Search any strain name and see typical terpene profiles, effects, and reviews. Filters stay put when you go back. Use this when you see an unfamiliar strain name.
Weedmaps
weedmaps.com ↗
Best for browsing multiple shops at once. Filter to Vape Cartridges and see what's in stock nearby. Wider shop coverage than Leafly, less terpene detail.

How to Use Leafly
1. Go to leafly.com → tap Dispensaries
2. Search Sterling Heights, MI
3. Tap a shop → tap Menu
4. Filter: Vape → Cartridge
5. Tap a strain → scroll to Terpenes
6. Is Caryophyllene first or second? → cross-check the Strains tab
How to Use Weedmaps
1. Go to weedmaps.com → allow location
2. Tap Dispensaries → pick a shop
3. Tap Menu → filter: Vape Cartridge
4. Tap unknown strain → look it up on Leafly separately
5. Use the site's back button — not Safari's — to keep filters

USE THIS AT THE COUNTER

Ask these four questions in order. If they can't answer Q1 and Q2, ask to see the COA — any reputable cart will have one.

Q1 — What's the dominant terpene?
Need caryophyllene 1st or 2nd. Walk away if it's limonene or pinene.
Q2 — Is limonene high or background?
Background = fine. Dominant = no.
Q3 — Fast or slow onset?
Slow = yes. Rocket = no.
Q4 — Can I see the COA?
Binske, Element, and Pleasantrees house all have one. Any good brand will.
